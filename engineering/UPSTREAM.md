instead of `just` recipes, Vite+ with Oxlint and Oxfmt instead of Biome, and the

imported, nor were Origin89's hardware, Embassy, Rust and publication material.
No external engineering synchronization or approval integration is installed. No
TypeSafe client, paid OCR workflow or agent automation is enabled by this import.
